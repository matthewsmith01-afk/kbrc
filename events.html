The season on the bay

From family fun runs to alpine half marathons, our calendar runs all year. Grab a date, tell a friend, and we will see you at the start line.

Flagship · June 27

The Spit Run 10K & Cosmic Hamlet Half Marathon

Our biggest day. Choose the 10K or the Cosmic Hamlet Half Marathon along the unforgettable Homer Spit. Runners start at 10:00 am; an untimed walkers division starts after 9:00 am. Proceeds benefit local youth running programs.

Distances: 10K & 13.1 Start: Homer High School Timing: Chip timed
